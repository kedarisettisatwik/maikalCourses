# Local textbook rewrite status

This is a content authoring task, not a title-renaming or synonym-replacement pass. No paid API is used.

## Reviewed batch included in 1.2.0

- **OOPS, Unit 1:** 25 original teaching levels replace the literal source chunks. The batch covers the eight core OOP concepts, language capabilities, procedural and object-oriented decomposition, benefits and limitations, application domains, real-time terminology, software-crisis concerns, all five development-life-cycle phases, and the model-based-testing case study's teaching point.
- These levels include short complete explanations, concrete examples, concept diagrams where useful, and one scenario checkpoint per level.
- **OOPS keyword definitions:** the supplied keyword list is paraphrased locally, with corrections for misleading historical wording. Original source wording remains in the original book.

The historical company names, old product feature tables, bibliography, and original exercises remain available in the supplied PDF. The rewritten learning path explains the concepts rather than copying the page furniture or news article.

## Remaining work

The exhaustive point-by-point rewrite is **not complete**. OOPS Units 2–14 and the seven other courses retain their full existing source learning trails. They have not been silently replaced by a much shorter set of companion summaries. All source transcripts and original PDFs remain intact.

Future local batches must read the relevant source chapter, cover its actual topic structure and definitions, build explanations and examples, check scenarios against what was taught, distinguish historical claims from current technical rules, and verify the lesson interface. A heading appearing in a coverage manifest is not proof that all of its substance has been rewritten.

`rewriteStatus` in each course JSON records reviewed chapter IDs and explicitly records that a complete point-by-point rewrite has not yet been achieved. `teachingSource` and `reviewedRewrite` record the status per chapter.

## Progress compatibility

Existing learning-stage IDs are retained for the rewritten OOPS foundation levels. Replaced summary-stage completions are archived locally; completing the entire previous chapter summary carries forward to the rewritten review set. Definitions, interviews, notes, page highlights, and unchanged course progress remain compatible. An unfinished lesson restarts at its preview when its lesson-flow edition changes.
